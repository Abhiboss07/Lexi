## 2. Scene-by-Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: The Hook & Brand Awakening (0.0s – 4.5s)
- **Visuals:**  
  Opens on tactile Light Sand `#F5EBD0` background. A soft radial glow expands. The Wine Red `#7F011F` Lexi dialogue logo draws onto the screen with a 4-point gold sparkle (✦) bursting outward.
- **Copy:**  
  - Main Heading: *"English Learning Reimagined."*
  - Supporting: *"No ads. No clutter. Just pure fluency."*
- **Motion:**  
  Smooth spring scale `[0.85 -> 1.0]`, subtle ease-out opacity.

### Scene 2: Guided Structure & Daily Flow (4.5s – 10.5s)
- **Visuals:**  
  Transition into dynamic 3D app showcase. The actual Lexi Home and Explore screens glide in with subtle depth shadow. Floating interactive cards highlight *"Daily Practice"*, *"CEFR A1–B2 Roadmap"*, and *"FSRS Spaced Repetition"*.
- **Copy:**  
  - Main Heading: *"Structured for Every Learner."*
  - Subtitle: *"Systematic Beginner Foundations to Intermediate Fluency."*
- **Motion:**  
  Smooth horizontal glide with 3D tilt perspective (`rotateY(-6deg)` to `rotateY(0deg)`).

### Scene 3: The AI Conversation Coach & Speaking Studio (10.5s – 16.0s)
- **Visuals:**  
  Deep dive into interactive practice. The Conversation Coach chat window animates active speech bubbles, progressive hint levels (1–3), and supportive linguistic feedback in rich Wine Red.
- **Copy:**  
  - Main Heading: *"Speak with Real Confidence."*
  - Subtitle: *"Live Voice Feedback & Instant Pronunciation Support."*
- **Motion:**  
  Staggered message reveals, pulse glow on the Wine Red microphone indicator.

### Scene 4: Outro & The Learner Invitation (16.0s – 20.0s)
- **Visuals:**  
  The screen centers on the full Lexi wordmark and icon. Three distinct value pillars badge in:
  `[ 100% Free ]` • `[ Private & Offline-First ]` • `[ Ad-Free ]`
  A primary Wine Red CTA button pulses gently: *"Start Learning Today"*.
- **Copy:**  
  - Title: *"Lexi"*
  - Subtitle: *"Your English Companion"*
  - Tagline: *"Free • Offline • Ad-Free"*
- **Motion:**  
  Gold sparkle celebration particles, button scale micro-bounce, and elegant fade to sand.
